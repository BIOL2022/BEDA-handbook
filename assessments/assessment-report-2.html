Report 2

This page contains the requirements for preparing and submitting your group dataset and individual Report 2, including the marking scheme.

Group data submission (5%)

Submit your group’s species (assemblage) and habitat data via Canvas. Only one group member needs to upload the files on behalf of the group.

  • Include species data and habitat data, including relevant factors.
  • Submit one Excel workbook (xls or xlsx), with both datasets on one worksheet or multiple worksheets, or two clearly labelled CSV files uploaded together.
  • Use consistent sample identifiers so the species and habitat records can be matched, and include the information needed to understand how the data were collected.
  • Make sure the data are clearly organised and correctly formatted for analysis in R or jamovi. A separate worksheet for each dataset is not required.
  • Submit by 10:00 am Sydney time (AEDT) on the day of your Week 11 practical session: Tuesday 20, Wednesday 21 or Friday 23 October 2026, according to your practical class.
  • This submission is worth 5% of your total unit mark.

Submit your group dataset in Canvas

Individual report (15%)

Write an individual short scientific report based on your group’s multivariate study. The emphasis is on explaining your analytical methods, accurately describing the data and presenting your results clearly and concisely. This report is worth 15% of your total unit mark.

Required report content

Include a Title, brief Abstract, Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, short Discussion, and References where used.

  • Introduction: state the study aim and hypotheses and briefly describe the study system. An imaginative ecological context is acceptable; a detailed real-world rationale or real examples are not required. Any references you use must be genuine and cited accurately.
  • Materials and Methods: describe your study design, data collection and analyses, including nMDS and PCA. Explain relevant variables, replication, analytical choices and software.
  • Results: explain the main data patterns and statistical findings, referring to your figures and tables. Include an nMDS plot, ANOSIM or PERMANOVA results, a PCA loadings table, and a scree plot. Present summary data rather than raw data, and do not use screenshots of figures or tables.
  • Discussion: interpret the findings in relation to the aims and hypotheses, discuss limitations, and suggest improvements or useful future work.

Use the marking scheme as a checklist before submission.

Format and page limit

Follow the format of a short scientific article suitable for Austral Ecology. The journal’s author guidelines are supplementary guidance; the assessment requirements on this page take precedence.

  • Maximum four single-spaced pages of report text.
  • The limit excludes the cover sheet, title/abstract page, references, tables, figures and AI-use declaration.
  • Include your tables, figures and declaration in the same submitted document.
  • Put the concise AI-use declaration after the references. Full chat transcripts and a detailed AI appendix are not required.

Submission

Submit anonymously via Canvas by Friday 6 November 2026 at 23:59 Sydney time (AEDT).

  • Upload one PDF, DOC or DOCX file.
  • Do not include your name in the report or the filename.

Submit your individual report in Canvas

Academic integrity and AI use

Complete the report independently and write it in your own words. Follow the University’s academic integrity guidance.

You may use AI tools for grammar and language assistance and coding/debugging support. You remain responsible for conducting and understanding your analyses, checking outputs, and producing your own scientific reasoning, interpretation and report. Do not substitute AI-generated work for these responsibilities.

After the references, include a concise declaration that:

  • names the tool or platform;
  • describes how it assisted you; and
  • summarises relevant prompts or gives short representative examples.

If you did not use AI, state that. The declaration is outside the four-page limit.
